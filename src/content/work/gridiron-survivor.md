## Problem

A community needed a reliable, self-serve way to run a weekly NFL
survivor pool — picks, scoring, and standings — without spreadsheets and
manual bookkeeping.

## Approach

Built a full-stack app in Next.js and TypeScript: authenticated picks,
automated scoring against live results, and a standings view the whole
league could follow in real time.

## Outcome

A working product used by a real community — and the project that turned
18 years of shipping in aerospace into a career in software.
